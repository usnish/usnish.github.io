Where 36 came from

What to say about 36

Cite 36 as a coverage rule copied from trial protocols, not as a threshold the dose-response curve located. The randomized evidence supports referring and enrolling; the observational curve can't tell you where the benefit stops.

What would settle it: a trial randomizing session count, or a target-trial emulation with negative-control outcomes.

How this was built

What this is. A simulation, not a reanalysis. No patient data are used. Every input is a published summary number (listed in published.json with DOI, metric, follow-up window and where in the paper it appears); the cohorts are synthetic. The simulation shows what is possible, not what is: its settings are assumptions, shown as sliders across a stated range, and a world with a real benefit fits Hammill's numbers as well as a world with none.
