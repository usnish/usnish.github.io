Can an AI write your prescription? One state, four pilots, and a wall of rules

Software already drafts prescriptions for clinicians to sign. The question now is how far past that the law lets it go. This page codes each law, rule, pilot and bill found on a four-rung ladder, from AI drafting a prescription for a clinician to sign, up to AI as the prescriber of record, and places each one on the rung it actually operates at today.

By Usnish Majumdar, MD · November 2026 · as of

      As of. Everything here was checked on or before . Utah's pilot register was last changed on ; bills are coded at their last recorded action.

      One coder. A single research pass by AI agents, with my review, coded every entry from a primary source where one could be opened. Nothing was coded twice independently. entries are rated high confidence and medium, usually because the primary text could not be opened and a secondary source was used. Each entry's confidence shows in its details.

      Two strengths of rule. An explicit ban names AI and bars it from prescribing, from reviewing prescriptions, or from holding a prescriber's license. A human-must-decide rule says a clinician keeps the decision, without naming prescriptions; it would bar rungs 2 to 4 only if a regulator read it that way. The list labels each one, and also separates laws and binding codes from guidance and from bills. Title and personhood laws are the weakest kind and are labeled as such.

      The China renewal point is unsettled.

      Context, not on the wall. entries describe frameworks that neither permit nor ban AI prescribing, such as the EU AI Act, FDA's decision-support guidance, the DEA rule that keeps controlled substances out of every pilot, and the sandboxes in Texas, Arizona and Wyoming, none of which lists an AI prescribing participant. Fujian's internet care rules, which copied China's national ban on AI-generated prescriptions, are also here rather than on the wall: they were valid for two years from February 2024 and no renewal was found. All are in the data file with the rest.
