# How much more Medicare pays for Medicare Advantage

A waterfall of MedPAC's estimate that Medicare will pay about 14% ($76B) more in 2026 for Medicare Advantage enrollees than traditional Medicare would have spent on them: payment rules (−1 point, net of plan bids), favorable selection (+11, contested) and coding intensity (+4). The 2025 estimate (20%, $84B) is marked for comparison.

**Live:** https://usnish.github.io/medicare-advantage-payments/

All figures are hand-entered in `data/figures.json`, each tagged with its source and whether it is reported or derived. Sources: MedPAC March 2026 Report to Congress ch. 12, MedPAC March 2025 ch. 11, KFF 2026 quality bonus estimate.
