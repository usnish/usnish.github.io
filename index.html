Who could use cardiac rehab? A county map

How common are the heart conditions that qualify patients for cardiac rehabilitation, county by county. Pick a measure; hover a county to compare all of them.

    About the data

      Eligibility. Medicare covers cardiac rehab after a heart attack, bypass surgery, angioplasty/stenting, heart valve repair or replacement, heart or heart–lung transplant, stable angina, and stable heart failure with reduced ejection fraction. The CR-eligible rate counts Million Hearts primary qualifying events (heart attack, bypass, PCI, valve procedure, transplant) among Medicare fee-for-service beneficiaries; the hospitalization rates are broader proxies for the same population.

      Caveats. Medicare measures cover fee-for-service beneficiaries 65+ only (Medicare Advantage enrollees are not included). Hospitalization rates are age-standardized and spatially smoothed by CDC. Coronary heart disease prevalence is a modeled self-report estimate for all adults; PLACES has no estimates for Kentucky or Pennsylvania in this release, and reports Connecticut by planning region, which doesn't match these county shapes. CDC labels the CR eligibility period both 2018–2019 and 2019–2020. Gray counties have no data or were suppressed for small numbers.
