# Medicare Advantage plans ending in 2027

Where Medicare Advantage members go next year according to the CMS plan crosswalk (stays in plan, moved automatically to another plan, plan leaving their county, plan ended), by county, insurer and plan type, for 2027 and, for comparison, 2026.

**Live:** https://usnish.xyz/ma-plan-exits/ (unlisted)

Sources: CMS Plan Crosswalk 2026 and 2027 (October 1 releases), CMS monthly enrollment by contract/plan/state/county (September 2025 and September 2026), CMS Plan Benefit Package files 2025–2027 (Section A plan details, PlanArea and PlanRegionArea service areas), CMS Landscape files CY2026 (September 2026 release) and CY2027 (November 2026 release) from https://www.cms.gov/medicare/coverage/prescription-drug-coverage.

Method: each plan-county enrollment cell follows its plan's crosswalk rows; a destination counts only if its next-year service area includes the county. Individual MA plans only (HMO, PPO, PFFS, including SNPs); employer plans, PACE, cost plans and MSAs are excluded. When the crosswalk lists both the plan itself (renewal) and another plan (consolidation) for a member's county, the member counts as staying: CMS splits these plans (all dual-eligible plans so far) by Medicaid enrollment, not county, and the crosswalk does not say who goes where (1,077,067 members for 2027, 664,893 for 2026; `members_split` in summary.json). Suppressed enrollment cells (1–10) count as 0. Every total, county count and table covers the 50 states and DC (territories are left out: 572,357 members for 2027).

Counties: one row per county on the site's county map (3,142), with zeros where a county has no members or no plans, so both cycles count the same counties. Three retired Virginia city codes that CMS still uses (51515 Bedford city, 51560 Clifton Forge, 51780 South Boston) are folded into Bedford, Alleghany and Halifax counties. Los Angeles has two SSA codes (05200, 05210); a plan serves a county if its service area lists any of the county's codes.

Insurers: grouped by the parent organization CMS lists per contract (H/R number), looked up first in the September enrollment file's contract list (one name per contract for both years of a cycle), then in that year's Landscape file for contracts that are new next year. Short display names come from `_viz/malib.py` `parent_name`, plus a few page-local names in `build_data.py` `DISPLAY` (MHH Healthcare = GlobalHealth in Oklahoma; Aware Integrated = Blue Cross and Blue Shield of Minnesota; BMC Health System = WellSense), checked against the CPSC contract file's marketing names. The insurer table shows the twelve largest insurers by membership plus any other with at least 100,000 members who must choose. Regional PPOs serve every county in their MA region (PlanRegionArea region codes; region-to-state map read from the Landscape file).

Rebuild: `python3 ma-plan-exits/scripts/build_data.py` (needs numpy and scipy for `_viz/vizlib.py`; e.g. `uv run --no-project --with numpy --with scipy python ma-plan-exits/scripts/build_data.py`).

## Checks

- Insurer counts per county match an independent count from the CMS Landscape files (parents of non-SNP MA plans; cost plans and MSAs left out; counties matched by name through the CPSC file) in all 3,142 counties for 2026 and 3,140 of 3,142 for 2027. The two that differ are Carroll and Coos, NH: Devoted Health was added there in the November 1 Landscape refresh, after the October 1 PBP files this page uses, so the page shows 0 insurers there for 2027 where CMS now lists 1. The build finds these from the Landscape file and flags them on the county card (`late_add_2027`). Both are in the 0–1 insurer count either way.
- The 2026 plans appear twice (next year in the 2026 cycle, previous year in the 2027 cycle); their plan and insurer counts agree in every county, and the 2027 view's previous-year 0–1 insurer count equals the 2026 view's (287). The build asserts both, and that the plan-type and insurer breakdowns add up to the headline totals.
- Outside benchmark, 2027 (KFF, "More Than 4 Million Medicare Beneficiaries Will Have Their Medicare Advantage or Stand-Alone Prescription Drug Plan Terminated at the End of 2026", Oct 27 2026, https://www.kff.org/medicare/more-than-4-million-medicare-beneficiaries-will-have-their-medicare-advantage-or-stand-alone-prescription-drug-plan-terminated-at-the-end-of-2026/):
  - Must choose: page 3,168,295 (10.8%); KFF about 3.1M. KFF counts MA drug plans only and uses June 2026 enrollment; the page uses all individual MA plans and September 2026 enrollment. Both count plans that end and plans that leave a county.
  - Plans open to anyone: page 2,850,566 must choose (13.4%); KFF 2.8M (14%) in general-enrollment MA drug plans.
  - Special needs plans: page 317,729 must choose (258,641 dual-eligible + 59,088 other); KFF about 300,000.
  - UnitedHealthcare: page 511,131 must choose (377,504 plan ended + 133,627 leaving county). UnitedHealth said about 390,000 members "will be impacted by plan closures" (spokesperson to Healthcare Brew, quoted in Fortune, Oct 20 2026: https://fortune.com/2026/10/20/unitedhealthcare-humana-ending-medicare-advantage-plans-for-nearly-1-million-people-open-enrollment/), close to the page's plan-ended count. KFF 367,000 (13%) in MA drug plans.
  - Humana: page 601,999 must choose (405,494 plan ended + 196,505 leaving county). Fortune (same article) reports Humana's CFO said on the July 2026 earnings call that about 600,000 people are in a Humana plan ending in 2027; that figure matches the page's must-choose total, not its plan-ended count, so the two company figures use different definitions. KFF 530,000 (19%).
  - Blue Cross and Blue Shield of Minnesota (Aware Integrated): page 258,328 must choose (90% of 286,842); KFF 249,000.
  - Moved automatically: page 2,092,756. KFF counts about 1.4M in consolidating plans. The difference is definitional. The page counts anyone whose crosswalk destination in their county has a different plan ID: 1,239,511 from consolidated renewals plus 853,245 in plans that renew under a new plan ID (627,922 Renewal Plan and 225,323 Renewal Plan with SAR rows pointing to another ID; `moved_by_status` in summary.json). KFF does not count the second group as moved, and does count the split plans the page treats as staying.

## Changes

- 2026-11-12 (second pass, review fixes):
  - Counties: every cycle now covers the same 3,142 map counties, with zeros for counties with no members or plans; before, a county counted only in a cycle where it had members or plans, so the 2027 view said 2026 had 197 counties with 0–1 insurers while the 2026 view said 216. The three retired Virginia city codes are folded into their counties. Counties with 0–1 insurers: 2027 419 (180 with none), was 287; 2026 287, was 214. Before: 329 (was 197) and 216 (was 143). Counties losing, gaining and losing an insurer are unchanged.
  - Moved vs stays: a member whose own plan renews in their county now always counts as staying. Before, the first serving row in the crosswalk file won, so 220,153 members in split dual-eligible plans counted as moved for 2027. 2027 moved 2,312,909 (7.9%) → 2,092,756 (7.1%); dual-eligible moved 22% → 19%. 2026 moved 1,568,480 → 1,482,553.
  - Los Angeles: both SSA codes now count. 2026 must choose 2,919,400 (10.3%) → 2,893,151 (10.2%); leaving 812,806 → 786,557 (Los Angeles 27,798 → 1,549). 2027 unchanged.
  - Plan-type and insurer tables now leave out territories like the headline (before, they included Puerto Rico, and MHH Healthcare's MCS Classicare made the top 12). The insurer table adds any insurer with 100,000 or more members who must choose (2027: Blue Cross MN / Aware Integrated; 2026: Lifetime Healthcare and UCare).
- 2026-11-12: Fixed insurer names. The parent company was looked up by contract and plan in the September enrollment file, so every plan ID that is new next year (about 1,000 plans in 2027) fell back to its marketing name. The same insurer then showed up as both leaving and new (e.g. "Aetna Medicare" new in 694 counties for 2027; HealthPartners leaving and new in Hennepin County, MN). The parent is now looked up by contract, with the Landscape file for contracts new next year. Regional PPOs, which the PBP files list by region rather than county, were missing from plan and insurer counts. They now count in every county of their region.
  - Effect, 2027: counties an insurer left 1,673 → 1,630, counties losing plans 2,205 → 2,236, gaining 512 → 469. Aetna (CVS) is now new in 63 counties (checked against the Landscape files, e.g. Cerro Gordo, IA). No county lists the same insurer as both leaving and new.
  - Effect, 2026: counties an insurer left 1,540 → 1,390, losing plans 1,832 → 1,871, gaining 819 → 798.
